# Onboarding & Offboarding

This document applies in full to staff. Contractors and creator partners are covered by §4 (access
provisioning) and §6 (deprovisioning and return of equipment), because those sections describe how
anyone gets a Westline account and how it is taken away again. See `HANDBOOK` §2.

## 1. Before the first day

### 1.1 What People & Culture does

The signed agreement is filed, payroll and the province of employment are set up, the home market is
recorded (`REMOTE` §1.1), the benefits waiting period clock is started (`BENEFITS` §2.2), and the
scheduling record is created with the correct tenure tier for paid-time-off accrual (`PTO` §1.1).

### 1.2 What the lead does

Confirm the start date and location, arrange the equipment order under §3 at least **10 business
days** ahead, raise the access request under §4 at least **5 business days** ahead, and name a buddy
for the first month.

### 1.3 Right to work and identity

Right-to-work documentation is verified before the first day. Contractors additionally provide a
business number and proof of liability insurance where the engagement requires it.

## 2. The first week — staff

Day one is orientation: the market office, the newsroom, the safety briefing, and equipment issue.
Within the first week: the `CONDUCT` briefing, the `INFOSEC` briefing including device enrolment and
the password manager, and the `EDITORIAL` briefing including the disclosure rules in `EDITORIAL` §4.
Field-going staff complete the `SAFETY` briefing before their first assignment, not within the first
week — it is a precondition to going out.

Within the first month: a paid-time-off and benefits session covering the 90-day waiting period so
that nobody discovers it at a dental appointment, and a first check-in with the lead.

## 3. Equipment — staff

### 3.1 Standard issue

A laptop, a phone or a phone allowance, and the role kit: a camera kit for field roles, an audio kit
for radio and podcast roles, a monitor for desk roles. Field kits are issued against a signed
equipment record.

### 3.2 Home office

The home-office allowance is in `EXPENSE` §4 and is claimed after the first day, not before.

### 3.3 Care and return

Equipment is Westline property, is not modified, and is not lent to anyone outside Westline. Loss or
damage is reported to the lead within 1 business day; damage in the course of work is not charged
back. Equipment is returned under §6.2.

## 4. Access provisioning

### 4.1 Timing

Accounts are provisioned within **2 business days** of an approved access request and are active from
the start date, not before. Requests are raised by the lead at least 5 business days ahead.

### 4.2 What is provisioned by class

| Class | Identity | Newsroom systems | Westline social handles | Source-material stores |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Staff | Full Westline identity | By role | By social editor approval | By assignment, `INFOSEC` §3 |
| Contractor | Contractor identity, expiring on the engagement end date | Read plus assignment-scoped write | Only with social editor approval | Only where the assignment requires it |
| Creator partner | Portal identity only | No | No | No |

Creator partner access is limited to the Creator Partnerships portal — briefs, deliveries and
invoicing under `CREATOR` §4.3. Creator partners are not given newsroom system accounts, and a
producer who needs to share material with a creator partner does so through the portal rather than by
provisioning access.

### 4.3 Least privilege and expiry

Access is granted to the least set that lets the person do the job, and every contractor and creator
partner identity carries an **expiry date** equal to the engagement end date. Extending an engagement
extends the identity; it is not open-ended.

### 4.4 Multi-factor authentication

Multi-factor authentication is enrolled before first sign-in for every class. `INFOSEC` §2.3.

## 5. Probation and early check-ins — staff

Staff serve a **6-month** probationary period with check-ins at 30, 90 and 150 days. Probation is
about fit and support, and a concern raised at 30 days should never be a surprise at 150. The
performance cycle in `PERF` begins after probation ends; probation check-ins are not performance
reviews and are not recorded as such.

## 6. Offboarding

### 6.1 Deprovisioning — same day

All access is revoked on the **last working day**, at the end of that day: identity, newsroom systems,
social handles, the password manager, the Creator Partnerships portal, and any shared mailbox or
distribution list. There is no grace period, and this applies identically to staff, contractors and
creator partners.

Where an engagement ends for cause, or where there is a security concern, access is revoked
**immediately** on notification rather than at the end of the day. `INFOSEC` §7.

### 6.2 Return of equipment

Westline equipment is returned within **5 business days** of the last working day, including loaner
equipment issued to creator partners under `CREATOR` §7.2. Returns are logged against the equipment
record. Unreturned equipment is charged at replacement cost where the agreement and provincial law
permit.

Equipment a creator partner bought themselves is theirs and is not returned — `CREATOR` §7.1 and
`EXPENSE` §7.2. There is no circumstance in which Westline asks a creator partner to hand over their
own camera.

### 6.3 Access during a long leave

Access is **suspended, not deleted**, for a leave of absence longer than 8 weeks, and restored on the
confirmed return date under `LEAVE` §7. Suspension is not offboarding and does not trigger equipment
return; equipment is retained through a leave unless the leave exceeds 26 weeks.

### 6.4 Final pay and records

Final pay includes accrued and unused paid time off under `PTO` §2.1. Benefits end on the last day of
the month of departure under `BENEFITS` §5.5. Final invoices from contractors and creator partners are
paid on their normal terms — Net 30 for creator partners under `CREATOR` §4.3.

### 6.5 Exit conversation

Offered to every departing staff member and contractor, conducted by a People & Culture partner rather
than by the lead, and optional. Themes are reported in aggregate; individual comments are not
attributed.

### 6.6 Continuing obligations

Confidentiality, source protection under `EDITORIAL` §7.1 and `INFOSEC` §3, and any exclusivity term
in a Creator Partner Agreement survive the end of the engagement. Source protection has no end date at
all.

## 7. Checklists

### 7.1 Staff onboarding

**Before day one.** Signed agreement filed. Payroll and province of employment set up. Home market
recorded (`REMOTE` §1.1). Benefits waiting period started (`BENEFITS` §2.2). Scheduling record created
with the correct tenure tier (`PTO` §1.1). Equipment ordered, 10 business days ahead (§1.2). Access
request raised, 5 business days ahead (§4.1). Buddy named. Right-to-work verified (§1.3).

**Day one.** Orientation, market office and newsroom introduction, safety briefing, equipment issue
against a signed record (§3.1), device enrolment and password manager (`INFOSEC` §2.4), multi-factor
authentication enrolled before first sign-in (§4.4).

**Week one.** `CONDUCT` briefing. `INFOSEC` briefing. `EDITORIAL` briefing including the disclosure
rules in `EDITORIAL` §4. `SAFETY` briefing **before** any field assignment, which is a precondition and
not a week-one item.

**Month one.** Paid time off and benefits session, covering the 90-day waiting period explicitly.
First lead check-in. Objectives set if joining before July 1 (`PERF` §5.1).

**Day 30, 90, 150.** Probation check-ins (§5).

### 7.2 Contractor onboarding

Engagement letter signed, with the claimable-cost terms it needs for `EXPENSE` §6.1. Business number
and proof of liability insurance where the engagement requires it (§1.3). Contractor identity
provisioned with an **expiry date equal to the engagement end date** (§4.3). Access scoped to the
assignment, not to the newsroom (§4.2). Multi-factor authentication enrolled. Device minimums
confirmed under `INFOSEC` §2.5. `SAFETY` briefing before any field work. `EDITORIAL` disclosure rules
confirmed — they bind contractors in full.

No paid-time-off record, no benefits enrolment, and no performance cycle: none of those policies apply
(`HANDBOOK` §2).

### 7.3 Creator partner onboarding

Creator Partner Agreement signed, with the tier recorded (`CREATOR` §1.3). Portal identity provisioned
— **portal only**, with no newsroom system access and no Westline social handle (§4.2). Invoicing set
up for Net 30 (`CREATOR` §4.3). Programme manager named. `EDITORIAL` §4 disclosure walkthrough, which
is the single most consequential briefing in the programme. `SAFETY` briefing, including `SAFETY` §5
if drone work is in scope. Loaner pool explained (`CREATOR` §7.2) alongside the fact that equipment
purchases are never reimbursable (`CREATOR` §7.1) — telling people both at once prevents most of the
programme's expense disputes.

### 7.4 Offboarding, any class

Last working day: all access revoked at end of day, or immediately where the departure is for cause or
there is a security concern (§6.1). Equipment returned within 5 business days, loaners included
(§6.2). Final pay including accrued unused time off for staff (`PTO` §2.1); final invoices on normal
terms for contractors and creator partners. Benefits end on the last day of the month of departure
(`BENEFITS` §5.5). Exit conversation offered (§6.5). Continuing obligations confirmed — confidentiality
and source protection, which has no end date (§6.6).

## 8. Common mistakes

**Access raised too late.** A request raised the day before a start date cannot be provisioned in
time; §4.1 needs 2 business days from approval and §1.2 asks for 5 business days from the lead.

**Access left over.** A contractor identity without an expiry date, or one extended informally when
the engagement was extended, is the most common finding in access reviews. §4.3 requires the expiry to
track the engagement end date.

**Equipment ordered against the wrong role.** A field role issued a desk kit turns up to a shoot
without a camera. Order against the role in the agreement, not the job title.

**A creator partner given newsroom access "just for this project".** Not permitted (§4.2). Material is
shared through the Creator Partnerships portal.

**Assuming benefits start on day one.** They start the first of the month after 90 days, and the
month-one session in §7.1 exists to say so before someone books a dentist.

**Treating a long leave as an exit.** Access is suspended, not deleted, and equipment is retained
(§6.3). Deprovisioning someone on parental leave is a real and avoidable error.

## 9. Changing class

Occasionally someone moves between workforce classes — a contractor is hired as staff, a staff member
leaves and returns as a creator partner. This is not an amendment to an existing record. It is an
offboarding under §6 followed by an onboarding under §1, with a new agreement, a new identity, and a
new applicability set under `HANDBOOK` §2.

Two things carry across and are easy to miss. Service for the benefits waiting period is credited
where someone moves directly from a Westline contractor or creator partner engagement of at least
twelve months into a staff role, so the waiting period is waived (`BENEFITS` §2.3). And source
protection obligations from the previous engagement continue unchanged, because they have no end date
(§6.6).

Everything else resets. A contractor becoming staff starts at Tier 1 for paid-time-off accrual unless
the employment agreement says otherwise (`PTO` §1.1), and a staff member becoming a creator partner
loses the group benefits on the last day of the month of departure (`BENEFITS` §5.5) while keeping the
assistance programme, which is open to every class (`BENEFITS` §6).
